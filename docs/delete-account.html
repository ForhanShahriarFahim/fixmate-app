Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to FixMate.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select “Delete account”.
  4. Review the notice, confirm, and enter your current password.

Cancel pending or accepted bookings first. In-progress, completion-pending, or disputed bookings must be resolved through support.

Request deletion without the app

Email fixmatebd.support@gmail.com from the email registered to your FixMate account. Use the subject “FixMate account deletion”. We may ask you to verify account ownership.

What is removed

Your authentication identity, personal profile fields, provider profile, services, messages you sent, and reviews are removed. This no-cost edition does not store uploaded media or device tokens. Historical bookings retain anonymized status and totals. A pseudonymous deletion marker and moderation identifiers may remain for security and abuse handling.
