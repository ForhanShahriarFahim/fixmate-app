Request deletion in the app

  1. Sign in to FixMate.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select “Request account deletion”.
  4. Review the notice, confirm, and enter your current password.

Cancel pending or accepted bookings first. In-progress, completion-pending, or disputed bookings must be resolved through support.

Request deletion without the app

Email fixmatebd.support@gmail.com from the email registered to your FixMate account. Use the subject “FixMate account deletion”. We may ask you to verify account ownership.

What happens next

The in-app request atomically disables marketplace access and creates an administrator work item. Because this Spark-plan release has no trusted cleanup server, the request does not itself erase your data or Authentication identity. A verified administrator performs the documented cleanup and updates the request state. The cleanup target includes your Authentication identity, personal profile fields, provider profile, active services, messages you sent, and reviews. This no-cost edition does not store uploaded media or device tokens. Historical booking status and limited anonymized moderation identifiers may remain for security, disputes, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.
