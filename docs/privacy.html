This policy describes how FixMate collects and handles information needed to operate and secure its home-service marketplace.

Information we collect

We collect account name, email, mobile number, adult and Terms confirmation, provider profile and service information, booking dates and addresses, cash-payment status, messages, reviews, reports, device notification tokens, and technical crash diagnostics.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, match bookings, release contact details after provider acceptance, coordinate service, send notifications, prevent overlapping provider bookings, moderate reports, provide support, and diagnose crashes.

Visibility and sharing

Approved provider profiles, active services, and reviews are public in the app. Full customer addresses and participant phone numbers are visible only to the booking participants after acceptance. Firebase and Google Cloud process data as service providers. FixMate does not sell personal information or use advertising SDKs.

Security and retention

Firebase Authentication, server-side Functions, App Check, Firestore Rules, Storage Rules, and role checks protect data. Messages, services, profiles, and reviews are removed during account deletion. Historical booking records retain only anonymized operational totals and status. Moderation records may be anonymized and retained for security and abuse handling.

Your choices

You may update your profile, block or report users, disable Android notification permission, or delete your account in the app. See the account deletion page for an external request path.
