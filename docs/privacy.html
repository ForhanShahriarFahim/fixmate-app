This policy describes how FixMate collects and handles information needed to operate and secure its home-service marketplace.

Information we collect

We collect account name, email, mobile number, adult and Terms confirmation, provider profile and service information, booking dates and addresses, cash-payment status, messages, reviews, reports, and technical crash diagnostics.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, match bookings, release contact details after provider acceptance, coordinate service, display in-app activity, prevent overlapping provider bookings, moderate reports, provide support, and diagnose crashes.

Visibility and sharing

Approved provider profiles, active services, and reviews are public in the app. Full customer addresses and participant phone numbers are visible only to the booking participants after acceptance. Firebase and Google Cloud process data as service providers. FixMate does not sell personal information or use advertising SDKs.

Security and retention

Firebase Authentication, App Check, Firestore transactions, Security Rules, and role checks protect data. FixMate does not upload user media in the no-cost edition. An in-app deletion request immediately disables marketplace access, but it does not itself erase data. A verified administrator must complete cleanup. The cleanup target includes the Authentication identity, personal profile fields, provider profile, active services, authored messages, and reviews. Historical booking status and limited moderation identifiers may be retained and anonymized for security, disputes, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. The deletion request record shows whether cleanup is requested, in progress, failed, or completed.

Your choices

You may update your profile, block or report users, or request account deletion in the app. See the account deletion page for the external request path and current limitations.
